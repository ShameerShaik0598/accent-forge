# AccentForge Curriculum

## Level 1: RP Clarity Foundations

Level 1 is designed for Indian English speakers who want practical, clearer RP pronunciation without turning the course into a dense phonetics textbook.

The order is intentional:

1. Non-rhotic R
2. BATH vowel
3. Schwa and weak forms
4. Clear final consonants
5. V and W contrast
6. TH sounds
7. Sentence stress
8. Pausing and thought groups
9. Linking R
10. Polite intonation
11. LOT, THOUGHT, and GOAT vowels
12. Shadowing mini dialogue

## Curriculum Principles

- Teach one accent concept at a time.
- Pair IPA with plain-language coaching.
- Include rhythm cues for every lesson.
- Use short sentences before longer shadowing passages.
- Prioritise transfer points common for Indian English speakers.
- Frame feedback as coaching, not diagnosis.

## Audio Production

Each lesson can be recorded in three layers:

1. Words or short phrases
2. Drill sentence
3. Shadowing passage

Reference files should be saved under:

```txt
public/audio/lessons/level-1/{lesson-slug}/
```

The lesson content already includes planned audio paths. If a file is missing, the UI still shows IPA, captions, and rhythm cues.
