# Level 1 Lessons Text for ElevenLabs

Use this as the recording/export checklist. Each item has a stable ID, the exact spoken text, and the MP3 path to add in VS Code.

Recommended recording style:

- Calm modern British RP or neutral southern British.
- Natural teaching pace, not theatrical.
- Record words and short phrases slowly but not robotically.
- Record drill sentences at normal learner-friendly speed.
- Leave short, natural pauses where the rhythm notes show `/`.
- Export MP3 files to the listed paths under `public/audio/lessons/`.
